❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What chains do you build on?

Primarily BNB Smart Chain and other EVM-compatible chains (Ethereum, Polygon, Base, Arbitrum). Ask us if you need something else.

Do you only do token launches?

No — we build and audit any kind of smart contract: DEX modules, staking, vesting, NFT contracts, DAOs, and full custom protocols.

How long does an audit take?

A single-contract audit typically takes 3–7 business days depending on complexity. Full ecosystem audits take longer — we'll give you a timeline after scoping.

Can you work alongside our existing team?

Yes. We regularly work alongside in-house developers, either leading the Web3 portion or reviewing their contracts before launch.

Is the pricing above final?

Those are starting points. Every project gets a fixed quote after a short discovery call based on actual scope.
